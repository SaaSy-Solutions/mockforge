This Privacy Policy explains how **SaaSy Solutions LLC** ("we", "us") collects, uses and shares personal data when you use MockForge Cloud (app.mockforge.dev, api.mockforge.dev, hosted mock endpoints and related services) and the mockforge.dev website. The self-hosted, open-source MockForge software does not send us any data unless you connect it to MockForge Cloud.

## 1. Data we collect

**Account data.** Name, username, email address, password hash, organization name and membership, role, and SSO identifiers if your organization uses single sign-on.

**Billing data.** Plan, billing interval, invoices and billing contact. Card details are collected and stored by Stripe; we never receive full card numbers.

**Customer Content.** API specifications, fixtures, scenarios, plugins, templates and other material you upload or create. If you place personal data in Customer Content, we process it on your behalf under our [Data Processing Agreement](/legal/dpa).

**Usage and log data.** Requests to hosted mocks and to our API (timestamps, paths, status codes, IP address, user agent), feature usage counts used for plan limits, and audit-log events such as sign-ins and settings changes.

**Diagnostic data.** Error and performance logs generated by our servers, which may include the endpoint involved, browser and device type, and your account identifier.

**Cookies and local storage.** An authentication session cookie, CSRF protection, and preferences such as theme and cookie consent. We do not use advertising cookies.

## 2. How we use data

- To provide, secure and support the Service, including authentication, hosted mocks, collaboration and billing.
- To enforce plan limits, prevent abuse and fraud, and investigate security incidents.
- To send service messages such as email verification, password resets, invoices, security notices and changes to these policies.
- To diagnose errors and improve reliability and performance.
- To comply with legal obligations.

Where the GDPR applies, our legal bases are performance of our contract with you, our legitimate interests in operating and securing the Service, compliance with legal obligations, and your consent where we ask for it.

We do not sell or rent personal data, and we do not use Customer Content to train machine-learning models. MockForge Cloud does not send Customer Content to third-party AI or LLM providers; AI features use only a provider key you supply yourself.

## 3. Sharing

We share personal data only:

- with the sub-processors listed in Section 7 of our [Data Processing Agreement](/legal/dpa), which host, store, secure, bill for and send email from the Service on our behalf under contractual confidentiality and security obligations;
- with other members of your organization, as your organization's settings allow;
- with AI or other third-party providers you configure yourself (for example, your own API keys), at your direction;
- when required by law or to protect the rights, safety and security of our users, the public or us;
- in connection with a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, subject to this policy.

## 4. International transfers

MockForge Cloud is hosted in the United States. If you access it from outside the US, your data is transferred to and processed in the US. Where required, we rely on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and equivalent safeguards.

## 5. Retention

- Account and Customer Content: kept while your account is active and deleted within 30 days of account deletion (backups age out within 90 days).
- Request logs and diagnostic data: generally kept for up to 90 days.
- Billing records: kept as long as tax and accounting law requires.
- Audit logs: kept for the retention period of your plan, then deleted.

## 6. Security

All traffic is encrypted in transit with TLS 1.2+, including the hop from Cloudflare's edge to our origin. Uploaded files and off-host database backups are stored in Cloudflare R2, which encrypts stored data at rest. The primary database runs on a dedicated server with restricted administrative access. We also use hashed passwords, scoped API tokens, role-based access control, tenant isolation, and audit logging. No system is perfectly secure; report suspected vulnerabilities to security@mockforge.dev.

## 7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to processing, and to withdraw consent. You can update most account data in Settings and delete your account there. For other requests, email privacy@mockforge.dev; we respond within 30 days. If your data is in Customer Content controlled by another organization, we will refer your request to that organization. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

California residents have the rights described in the CCPA/CPRA, including to know, delete and correct personal information. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

## 8. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

We will post changes here and update the date above. For material changes we will notify account holders by email or in the app before they take effect.
